About Dhara

Dhara helps you track and prepare. It does not diagnose or replace a doctor.

Small ideas

After you log, Dhara may show one gentle, optional idea, at most once a day. They are general ideas, not medical advice, and never about weight or calories. They have not been reviewed by a clinician yet.

Safety and help

Dhara helps you track and prepare. It can’t tell you what is wrong.

When to get medical care

Please see a doctor soon, or go to a health facility, if you have:

  • Very heavy bleeding (soaking a pad or tampon every hour for two hours or more), or bleeding with dizziness or fainting.
  • Severe pain in your lower belly, or pain with a fever.
  • A missed period with pain or bleeding, if you could be pregnant.
  • No period for 90 days or more.

For chest pain or trouble breathing, call an ambulance (102) or go to the nearest hospital right away.

Feeling very low or unsafe

You don’t have to face this alone. If you might act on thoughts of harming yourself, or you are in danger, call the police (100) or an ambulance (102), or go to the nearest hospital.
